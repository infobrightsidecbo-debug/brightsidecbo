Who We Are

A Community, For the Community

Brightside CBO is a registered non-governmental, not-for-profit Community Based Organisation rooted in Kajiado Central. We were born out of an urgent need to address the growing crisis of drug and substance abuse devastating families and young people across Kajiado County.

Over time, our vision expanded. We recognised that sustainable change means tackling root causes — poverty, lack of education, climate vulnerability, and limited health access. Today, Brightside CBO runs five integrated programmes that work together to build resilient, self-reliant communities.

We are proudly inclusive: our programmes actively involve persons with disabilities (PWDs), communities from all tribal backgrounds, and people of all faiths. Every person belongs here.

Drug & Substance Abuse Prevention

Our founding mission. We run awareness campaigns, counselling outreach, rehabilitation referrals, and community dialogues to protect young people and families from the devastating effects of substance abuse across Kajiado County.

AwarenessCounsellingOutreach

Women & Youth Economic Empowerment

We have supported 200+ women and youth to start and grow their own small businesses through skills training, mentorship, and business development support — building lasting financial independence across Kajiado.

Business SkillsEntrepreneurshipMentorship

Climate Change Resilient Actions

Kajiado is on the front lines of climate change. We equip communities with adaptive farming, environmental conservation, and climate-smart livelihoods to protect people and the land they depend on.

ConservationAdaptationLivelihoods

Health Access & Promotion

We facilitate health camps, reproductive health awareness, mental health support, and linkages to healthcare services — ensuring communities in Kajiado Central access quality care regardless of background.

Where We Are

Find Us in Ilbisil Town

Brightside CBO is headquartered in Kajiado Central, in the heart of Kajiado County — a region facing unique challenges including pastoralist livelihoods under climate stress, youth unemployment, and substance abuse.

Our office is conveniently located along the main highway, accessible to all communities we serve across the county.
